# 03 · Forebrain overview

The forebrain grey matter (`FGM`) has three children:

```
FGM gray matter of forebrain
├── Tel  telencephalon        (1267)  the "end brain": both hemispheres
│   ├── Cx  cerebral cortex   (1100)  the outer folded sheet          → 04, 05
│   └── CN  cerebral nuclei   ( 165)  deep grey masses of the hemisphere → 06
├── Die  diencephalon         ( 190)  the "between brain", in the centre → 07
│   ├── THM    thalamus
│   ├── SubTH  subthalamus
│   ├── HTH    hypothalamus
│   └── Ret    retina  (the eye's retina grows out of the diencephalon!)
└── FTS  transient structures ( 651)  fetal zones                     → 11
```

## Telencephalon vs diencephalon

| | Telencephalon (Tel) | Diencephalon (Die) |
|---|---|---|
| Where | the two large hemispheres | the core, between the hemispheres and around the third ventricle |
| Made of | cortex (a layered sheet) and cerebral nuclei (deep masses) | nuclei only |
| Role | higher functions: perception, language, memory, planning, emotion, voluntary movement | relay and regulation: the thalamus filters information going to the cortex, the hypothalamus controls the body's internal state |

## The cerebral cortex is split by **age** first, then by **lobe**

```
Cx cerebral cortex
├── NCx   neocortex (isocortex)  (766)  6 layers, the "new" cortex, ~90% of the surface → 04
├── PACx  periallocortex         (172)  transition cortex: entorhinal, cingulate border → 05
└── ACx   allocortex             (159)  "old" cortex, 3 layers: hippocampus, olfactory → 05
```

This is the evolutionary classification used by neuroanatomists. The usual
"frontal / parietal / temporal / occipital" lobes appear **one level lower**, inside the neocortex.

## The cerebral nuclei

```
CN cerebral nuclei
├── BN   basal nuclei (basal ganglia): striatum + globus pallidus
├── AMY  amygdaloid complex
├── ATA  amygdaloid transition areas
├── EXA  extended amygdala (BNST)
├── Cla  claustrum
└── BF   basal forebrain (septum, nucleus basalis of Meynert)
```

**Watch out:** in Allen the **amygdala is a cerebral nucleus**, not part of the cortex or of the
limbic lobe. Details are in [06](06_cerebral_nuclei.md).
