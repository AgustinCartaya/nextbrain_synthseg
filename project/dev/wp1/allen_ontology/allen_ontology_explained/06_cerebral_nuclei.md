# 06 · Cerebral nuclei (CN)

These are the deep grey masses inside each hemisphere.

```
CN cerebral nuclei (165)
├── BN   basal nuclei (basal ganglia)  (34)
│   ├── STR  striatum
│   │   ├── Ca   caudate nucleus  (head CaH, body CaB, tail CaT)
│   │   ├── Pu   putamen
│   │   └── NAC  nucleus accumbens (core, shell)
│   └── GP   globus pallidus  (GPe external, GPi internal, VeP ventral pallidum)
├── AMY  amygdaloid complex  (65)
│   ├── BLN  basolateral group (La lateral, BL basolateral, BM basomedial, PL paralaminar)
│   ├── CEN  central group     (CEm, CEl)
│   ├── CMN  corticomedial group (Co cortical nuclei, Me medial, AHA)
│   └── AAA, En, INA, ...
├── ATA  amygdaloid transition areas (to hippocampus, striatum, cortex)
├── EXA  extended amygdala  (BNST bed nucleus of the stria terminalis, SLEA)
├── Cla  claustrum (dorsal, ventral, temporal)
└── BF   basal forebrain
    ├── SEP  septal nuclei (medial, lateral, ...)
    ├── BNM  basal nucleus of Meynert
    ├── NDB  nucleus of the diagonal band
    └── SI, IsCj (islands of Calleja), NSP
```

## Functions

| Structure | Function |
|---|---|
| **Caudate** | goal-directed behaviour, learning, eye movements, cognitive loops with the prefrontal cortex |
| **Putamen** | motor habits and automatic movements; together with the caudate it forms the *dorsal striatum*, which is damaged in Parkinson's (loss of dopamine input) and Huntington's disease |
| **Nucleus accumbens** | reward, motivation, pleasure, addiction (the *ventral striatum*) |
| **Globus pallidus** | the main output of the basal ganglia: lets wanted movements through and blocks unwanted ones (GPi is a target of deep brain stimulation) |
| **Ventral pallidum** | the output of the reward circuit |
| **Amygdala: basolateral** | learning what is dangerous or rewarding (e.g. fear conditioning) |
| **Amygdala: central** | the fear response: freezing, heart rate, stress hormones |
| **Amygdala: medial / cortical** | social and olfactory signals |
| **BNST (extended amygdala)** | sustained anxiety; stress |
| **Claustrum** | a thin sheet connected with almost all the cortex; proposed to coordinate attention and awareness (still debated) |
| **Septal nuclei** | the medial septum generates the hippocampal *theta rhythm* (memory); the lateral septum is involved in reward and aggression |
| **Basal nucleus of Meynert** | the main source of **acetylcholine** for the cortex: attention and memory; degenerates in Alzheimer's disease |
| **Diagonal band** | cholinergic input to the hippocampus and the olfactory system |

## Common confusions

- **Basal ganglia in the clinical sense = BN + subthalamic nucleus + substantia nigra.** In Allen
  these are in three different places:
  - BN under CN (here);
  - the subthalamic nucleus under the diencephalon (07);
  - the substantia nigra under the midbrain (08).
- **The amygdala is not in the cortex branch.** The limbic lobe (`LimL`) is under the surface
  structures. See 11.
