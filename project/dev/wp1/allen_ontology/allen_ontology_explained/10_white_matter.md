# 10 · White matter (FWM, MWM, HWM)

White matter is **cables**, not processors: bundles of axons that connect the grey-matter regions.
Allen gives every tract a **lowercase acronym**, and lists them by the vesicle they pass through.
A long tract is therefore split into portions, e.g. `ml-m` (medial lemniscus, midbrain portion) and
`ml` (in the hindbrain).

## Forebrain white matter: two families

```
FWM white matter of forebrain (139)
├── FCFT commissural fibres  → connect the LEFT and RIGHT hemispheres
│   cc corpus callosum, ac anterior commissure, hic hippocampal commissure, hac habenular commissure
└── FIFT ipsilateral fibres  → stay on the SAME side (association + projection fibres, ~75 tracts)
```

The main tracts and what they connect:

| Tract | Type | Connects / function |
|---|---|---|
| **cc** corpus callosum | commissural | the two hemispheres; the largest tract in the brain |
| **ic** internal capsule | projection | cortex ↔ thalamus, brainstem and spinal cord (motor and sensory highway); a small stroke here can paralyse half of the body |
| **cor** corona radiata | projection | the fan of fibres above the internal capsule |
| **fx** fornix | limbic | hippocampus → mammillary bodies and septum (memory) |
| **cb** cingulum bundle | association | along the cingulate gyrus, cingulate ↔ parahippocampal (memory, emotion) |
| **slf / af** superior longitudinal / arcuate fasciculus | association | frontal ↔ parietal and temporal; **language** (Broca ↔ Wernicke), spatial attention |
| **ilf** inferior longitudinal fasciculus | association | occipital ↔ temporal (recognising objects and faces) |
| **unf** uncinate fasciculus | association | orbitofrontal ↔ temporal pole (emotion, social memory) |
| **off** occipitofrontal fasciculus | association | occipital ↔ frontal |
| **ot / ox / or** optic tract, chiasm, radiation | sensory | the visual pathway: eye → LGN → V1 |
| **extC / extrC** external / extreme capsule | association | thin sheets around the claustrum |
| **mtt** mammillothalamic tract | limbic | mammillary body → anterior thalamus (memory) |
| **st** stria terminalis | limbic | amygdala → BNST and hypothalamus |
| **mfb** medial forebrain bundle | modulatory | dopamine and reward fibres |

## Midbrain and hindbrain white matter

| Tract | Function |
|---|---|
| **cpd** cerebral peduncle → **py** pyramidal tract → **xpy** pyramidal decussation | voluntary movement: cortex → spinal cord. The tract crosses in the medulla, which is why the left brain moves the right body |
| **cpn** cortico-pontine fibres | cortex → pontine nuclei → cerebellum |
| **scp / mcp / icp** superior / middle / inferior cerebellar peduncle | the cerebellum's cables: output (scp), input from the cortex via the pons (mcp), input from the spinal cord and inferior olive (icp) |
| **ml** medial lemniscus | fine touch and position, to the thalamus |
| **ll** lateral lemniscus | hearing, to the inferior colliculus |
| **mlf** medial longitudinal fasciculus | coordinates the two eyes and the head |
| **ctg** central tegmental tract | red nucleus → inferior olive (motor learning loop) |
